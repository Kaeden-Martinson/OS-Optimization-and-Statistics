### What's new

**Switch Strip and Panel from the overlay.** A small layout button at the right end of the Strip, and in the Panel's header, switches between the two layouts with one click. Your game keeps focus, and the rest of the overlay still lets clicks through to it. If a game on your list has its own layout, the button changes that game's layout. Don't want it? Turn off **Layout button on the overlay** on the Overlay page.

Also: updates now come from this repository's new name, github.com/Kaeden-Martinson/Optiframe (the old address still redirects).

If you're on 2.1.0 or 2.1.1, Settings › Check now offers this update.
