Moving and pinning the overlay

How it would work, step by step (Strip shown; Panel behaves the same). Tell me if any step should work differently.

1. Unlock

Tray menu "Move overlay", or the button on the Overlay settings page. The overlay gets a dashed outline and a handle bar; clicks stop passing through to the game.

3. Pin

Press Pin: the outline and handle disappear, clicks pass through to the game again, and the spot is saved (also per screen, so it comes back after a restart or a resolution change).

4. Corners stay as presets

On the Overlay settings page, the four corners (and "Custom" for a pinned spot) are one click. Picking a corner moves it there and pins it.
