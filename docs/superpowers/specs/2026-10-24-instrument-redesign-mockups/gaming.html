Pipeline: the four steps gaming mode takes, left to right

Gaming mode is laid out as what actually happens: detect the game, change power, boost, restore. Each step holds its own switch or timing, so the delays sit next to the step they control. It explains itself; it's a little less compact.

Timeline: your last session, showing when it switched

A real-looking session drawn as a timeline: when the game was in front, when gaming mode came on (5 s later), the Alt+Tab it ignored, and when it switched back (20 s after you left). The settings sit under it in two columns. This is the clearest picture of the timings; it takes the most space.
